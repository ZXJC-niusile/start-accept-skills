# Scientific review

Own the scientific assessment; keep the manuscript read-only. Use [shared principles](../../shared/CONTRACT.md) and [evidence guidance](../../shared/EVIDENCE.md); load [issue records](../../shared/ISSUES.md) when creating a ledger.

1. Resolve the supplied manuscript/version, available supplements, paper type, desired depth, and target criteria. Author-owned work needs no mandatory intake form. For third-party confidential review, establish the user's authority and the venue's AI, confidentiality, and assistance restrictions before processing. Record uncertain policy rather than treating authorship as venue permission.
2. Map the research question, contribution, central claims, assumptions, and supporting evidence. Review available relevant sections and supplements; disclose partial coverage. Separate what the manuscript reports from code observations and actual experiment measurements.
3. Load [scientific criteria](references/scientific-criteria.md). Assess only applicable criteria for methods, systems, benchmarks, or theory. Verify high-impact novelty objections against primary sources with public-safe queries; unavailable verification remains uncertain.
4. For each substantive concern, check the strongest plausible defense before deciding. Distinguish missing reporting from demonstrated error. Give the smallest adequate remedy and a verifiable close criterion. Do not demand experiments unrelated to the central claim or automatically penalize theory for lacking experiments.
5. Use optional role-separated reviewers only when requested or useful within authorized delegation. Load [panel discipline](references/panels.md) then. No fixed reviewer count, mandatory model substitution, or claim of independent errors. A single reviewer can finish the task.
6. Match the requested depth. A focused comment can be short prose with its evidence and uncertainty. For a structured review, use [report template](templates/report.md); follow the issue record rules when findings will be tracked or verified later. Do not invent balance or consensus among reviewers.

Recommendations are drafts or simulations. Do not submit them or contact others.

Use [paper writing](../sa-paper-writing/SKILL.md) for requested prose changes, [revision check](../sa-revision-check/SKILL.md) for prior issues or version comparisons, and [submission check](../sa-submission-check/SKILL.md) for formatting/compliance. Use [code audit](../sa-code-audit/SKILL.md) or [experiment analysis](../sa-experiment-analysis/SKILL.md) when supplied artifacts need deeper inspection.

Finish when the requested scope has anchored strengths/concerns, explicit unknowns, actionable next steps, and an honest coverage statement. A score is optional, uses a stated rubric, and never implies calibrated acceptance probability.
